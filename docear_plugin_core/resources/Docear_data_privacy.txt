Twigmark Data Privacy (local-first)

Twigmark is designed to keep your data on your computer.

1. What stays local
   Mind maps (.mm), settings, clipboard history databases, MCP audit logs, pomodoro stats, and similar product data are stored in directories you choose (home directory / application data). They are not uploaded to docear.org.

2. Network
   The application does not contact docear.org for status or sync. Optional help links may open the project GitHub page in your browser when you click them. Features that call third-party AI or tools (if you enable them) are under your control and their own privacy policies.

3. No registration server
   There is no Mantou registration or analytics server built into Twigmark for this release.

4. Contact
   https://github.com/yixiaozi/docear-desktop
